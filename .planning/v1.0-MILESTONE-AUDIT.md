## Executive Summary

The v1.0 milestone has shipped a comprehensive economic simulation engine across phases 1-11: fractional-reserve banking, capital markets, fiscal budget execution, inflation dynamics with Taylor Rule, an Economy Tab in DesignReview, real-world location bootstrap from World Bank data, multi-scenario simulation forking, immersive prompts split across 7 modules, enterprise-mediated payroll, structural pressure (cortisol/happiness from macro conditions), fiscal escrow, tax withholding at 5 hook sites, governance toggle, taxPolicy choice, and SFC subsystem drift telemetry with a dashboard alert banner.

The `gaps_found` status is driven by:

1. **Phase 12 (Labor Market Realism) is entirely unexecuted.** All 7 plans authored, 0 executed. The exact symptom that motivated the phase — China bootstrap producing 100% PRODUCE_AND_SELL agents — remains present in the deferred items of Phase 11.
2. **Phase 8 (Live Scenario Comparison) shipped 6 of 8 plans;** 08-06 and 08-07 were explicitly skipped, leaving LSC-03 (live multi-stream SSE), LSC-04 (multi-provider load balancer wiring), and LSC-09 (combined policy brief) unsatisfied. `getLoadBalancer()` is implemented and tested but never called.

4. **7 phases lack a top-level VERIFICATION.md** (01, 02, 04, 05, 07, 08, 10). Phase 03 has one but it pre-dates the gap-closure plans that fixed its issues. Phase 11 has only a gap-closure verification.

None of these gaps represent broken core functionality — the simulation engine runs, all 24 GC5 acceptance criteria pass, the live smoke test on US bootstrap is signed off, and the production code paths for shipped phases are wired correctly. The audit fails because the milestone is incomplete: Phase 12 has not started, and the previous audit's findings have not been closed.
