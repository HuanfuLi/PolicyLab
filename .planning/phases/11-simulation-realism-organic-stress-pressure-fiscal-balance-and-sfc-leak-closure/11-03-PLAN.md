<objective>
Redirect infrastructure, education, and defense fiscal spending from per-agent transfers into a per-session public-goods escrow ledger per D-10 and D-11. Welfare continues to distribute to agents unchanged. Escrow fiat stays inside the SFC perimeter, counted in `computeSystemFiatTotal`, so M0 remains constant. Quality-score side effects on stats (productivity, skill, enforcement multipliers) remain unchanged — spending still buys quality, it just parks the fiat rather than destroying it through disbursement.

Purpose: Close the fiscal leak where infra/edu/def spending was effectively mint-to-agents (creating emergent utopia bias). Escrow converts them into genuine "government-held" fiat matching the `sessionStateTreasury` precedent.
Output: Extended FiscalDelta with `escrowDeltas`; per-session in-memory escrow map synced to session.config; runner update wiring escrow into SFC accounting; passing sfcEscrow test.
</objective>
