## Logic

Two complementary signals fire when `foodSpotPrice > baseline × 1.5` OR `foodReserve < 30`:

1. **Personal dashboard** (agent-intent.ts): "Food is scarce and prices are high. Farming (PRODUCE_AND_SELL) would earn you ~{yield × price} fiat right now — extremely profitable."
2. **Market intelligence** (simulationRunner.ts): "⚠ OPPORTUNITY: Food reserves are CRITICAL/LOW and prices are X fiat/unit. Farming yields ~20 units worth ~Y fiat — one of the most profitable actions available right now."

Baseline price constant `FOOD_BASELINE_PRICE = 6.0` matches `FOOD_SPOT_BASELINE` in simulationRunner.ts.
