**Design decisions locked:**
- `enterpriseOwnerId` is an agent.id (DB-persisted), NOT the in-memory enterpriseId — survives export/import (Pitfall 6).
- Corporate bond `issuerId` = agent.id of enterprise owner (same rationale).
- Dividends come from owner's wealth directly — no separate enterprise retained_earnings account (SFC-trivial, recommended by research).
- No `bondEscrow` in SFC — bond purchase is a wealth transfer between buyer and issuer, both already in the SFC perimeter (Pitfall 1 from research).
